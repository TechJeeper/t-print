T-Print

Flash your T‑Embed in one click

  1. Plug in the T‑Embed over USB‑C (Chrome or Edge on desktop).
  2. Install firmware, then connect your phone to T-Print Wi‑Fi.
  3. Open http://192.168.4.1, join your home Wi‑Fi, then reopen setup from the IP shown on the device.
Use Chrome or Edge on desktop for Web Serial flashing. Serve this folder over http://localhost to allow Web Serial.

Icons from Iconify mdi. After flashing, setup stays on-device — no cloud account.
